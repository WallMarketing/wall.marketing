Set up your Wall Marketing display

This connects to a display over Bluetooth so you can give it your WiFi details — no need to plug it into a computer.

This browser can't do that.
Bluetooth setup only works in Chrome or Edge, on a computer or an Android phone. Safari, iPhone/iPad, and Firefox aren't supported yet — please switch browsers or devices to continue.

1Connect to the display

Make sure you're standing near the display, then tap the button below and choose it from the list that appears. It'll show up with a name like "WallDisplay-XXXXXXXXXXXX".

2Choose your network

These are sent directly to the display over Bluetooth — they don't go through this website or get stored anywhere else.
