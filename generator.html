What the panel can print.

The screen mixes six pigments and nothing else. Every generated slot is snapped to these inks, so the preview above is what goes on the door.

Artwork
PNG at 600 × 400 pixels, landscape
Panel
4-inch E Ink Spectra 6, 84.6 × 56.4 mm visible, 180 dpi
Colours
Black, white, red, yellow, blue and green pigment
Refresh

Lead time
24 hours from approved artwork to first play

Why the artwork looks like this

E-paper prints rather than glows. There is no backlight to carry a dim colour, so flat blocks of one ink read cleanly and gradients turn to grain.

  • Anything between the six inks is mixed by dithering, which shows as speckle at arm's length.
  • Thin strokes and small type break up. Nothing below about two pixels survives.
  • Photographs reproduce poorly. Drawn shapes and heavy type hold at four inches.
  • One idea per slot. A second line of explanation belongs somewhere else.
