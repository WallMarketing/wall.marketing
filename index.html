Design for four inches

This is a small screen read from close up, so it behaves like a page rather than a poster. One idea per slot, six or seven words at most, and type heavy enough to hold at a glance. Anything needing a second line of explanation belongs somewhere else.

It is e-paper, so it prints rather than glows: no backlight, readable in any light, and flat six-colour artwork reproduces far better than gradients or photographs. Send your brand colours over early and we will tell you how they land.

Your slot lands between the headlines and the forecast, so it is read in the same frame of mind. Copy that sounds like information travels further here than copy that sounds like an advert.
