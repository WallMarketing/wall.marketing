A printed page that changes once a day. Nothing more.

The unit contains no camera, no microphone and no sensor of any kind. Nothing about the people in front of it is seen, recorded or transmitted.

The screen is electronic paper, not a backlit panel. It emits no light, makes no sound, never flickers and never moves; it holds its image without drawing any power at all.

Your visitors are already looking at this door. We simply give them something worth reading.

No power. No wiring. No work.

PowerNone required

Integrated solar cells harvest your existing room lighting; that is enough for the single update the screen makes each day. There is no wired power connection, no cable, no plug and no electrical inspection. Nothing is added to your power bill.

NetworkA few kB per day

Content reaches the unit over your guest network, a few kilobytes a day. The device cannot see or reach anything else on your network. A full IT security summary is available on request.

Where network access isn’t permitted, as in many healthcare, aviation and financial settings, we supply a cellular version that connects independently and asks nothing of your IT team at all.

FittingUnder 60 seconds per unit

The installer lifts the backing from the high-strength adhesive mount and positions the unit; almost all of that minute is spent on alignment rather than fitting. No drilling, no fixings, no damage to the door, no contractor and nothing taken out of service.

A whole site is typically finished in a single visit, outside your busiest hours if you prefer.

CleaningOne wipe

The housing comes in high-strength engineered plastic or brushed metal to suit your interior, treated to resist bacterial growth on the surface of the unit. Edges are rounded and there are no flat ledges to collect dust or moisture. A single wipe is all your cleaning team ever needs to do.

UpkeepOurs, not yours

No servicing, no consumables and no involvement from your staff whatsoever. Each unit reports its own status to us, so on the rare occasion one needs attention we usually know before you do, and we deal with it at our cost. Any unit damaged or stolen is replaced at our expense.

RemovalNo trace

If you decide the wall units aren’t right for your site, they lift away and a solvent wipe leaves each door exactly as we found it. We collect at our expense, whenever you ask.
